# Video Skills

> Make great videos about your product 10x faster.

I used to spend hours recording and editing videos in Screen Studio. Now, I can make a video in 30 minutes.

> Storyboard-first approach

The hardest part of a product video is matching the visuals to what you're saying. 

With a storyboard, you settle the
story before you record, so you only have to talk. Your agent handles the visuals and the editing.

## Skills

- **narrated-video**: demos, tutorials, and launch videos, narrated by you over your real app.
- **teaser-video**: short, punchy clips with no voiceover, cut to music.

## Install

```sh
npx skills add every-app/video-skills
```

## How it works

1. Dictate your idea for the video.
2. Iterate with your agent to turn it into a storyboard.
   - Ask your agent to add screenshots of your app.
3. Record yourself talking over the storyboard, with something like QuickTime Player.
4. Give your agent the recording.
5. Your agent creates a draft of the video, animated as if you were using the app yourself.
6. Record a couple more takes and iterate on the visuals.
7. Post awesome videos for your customers and fans.
